# InventoryOS: learn the daily workflow

InventoryOS helps you track what you own, where it is, what sold, and what to do next. Start with one item and one storage location, then try the later chapters when you need them.

> **In this guide:** Screenshots are captured from the current InventoryOS app preview with fictional products, accounts and orders. Your screen may show a different theme, workspace layout or location name.

## Pick your starting point

| Plan | What you will learn |
| --- | --- |
| **Intro · £0** | Dashboard, locations, add/search/move/remove stock, basic stale-stock checks. Up to 100 active stock items. |
| **Premium · £7.99/month** | Receipts and expenses, full sales history, corrections/refunds, barcode labels, saved print jobs, and Dispatch Centre. Up to 1,000 active items. |
| **Pro · £19.99/month** | Advanced stock and dispatch, reordering with reminder emails, shared workspaces, manufacturing, and your first physical store. Each additional physical store is £10/month. |

Check **Settings → Plans & billing** for your account’s plan and store allowance. Existing subscriptions keep their agreed billing until you choose to change.

### A five-minute first run

1. Open [Dashboard](https://app.inventoryos.co.uk/index.html) and check the workspace and inventory selectors at the top.
2. Open [Boxes / Locations](https://app.inventoryos.co.uk/boxes.html) and create your first storage location.
3. Open [Add Stock](https://app.inventoryos.co.uk/add.html), enter a description, quantity, and location, then select **Save Product**.
4. Open [Search Stock](https://app.inventoryos.co.uk/search.html) and find the item by name or barcode.
5. Follow [Moving and removing stock](free/manage-stock.md) when the item moves or sells.


*Check the workspace and inventory selectors above the stock cards before making stock changes.*
