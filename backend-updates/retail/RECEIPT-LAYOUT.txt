INVENTORYOS RETAIL — RECEIPT UPDATE (v14)

For an existing Retail/email-receipt installation. No Stripe setup needs repeating.

1. Finish any open till baskets and sync offline sales. Stop the backend using
   your usual method before installing. Keep your normal database backup.
2. Extract the ZIP. Put the complete InventoryOS-receipt-layout-update folder
   inside your backend folder, beside server.js. Do not copy the individual files.
3. Open PowerShell in your backend folder and run:

   node .\InventoryOS-receipt-layout-update\install.cjs
   node .\check-retail.cjs

4. If the check passes, restart InventoryOS Backend using your usual method.
5. Refresh Retail and the Till app (close and reopen installed apps if needed).
6. Make a sandbox sale and print/email its receipt to check your shop details.

WHAT CHANGED
- Shop display name heads sale, return, deposit and wastage receipts and loyalty
  cards. Change it in Admin > Store location > Shop display name.
- Customer receipts show the saved cashier login number, without staff names.
  Internal staff records and audit history remain available to the owner.
- Email sender name/subject use the shop display name. The verified sending
  email address stays the same.
- Emails now resemble narrow paper till receipts: monochrome, monospaced,
  itemised lines, dashed separators and a clear total.
- VAT still uses the registered business details when enabled. Business name
  (VAT details) is the separate field for those details.
- New sale snapshots keep the shop display name at the time of sale, including
  offline sales. Older receipts without a shop snapshot use that shop's current
  display name. If an old sale has no saved cashier number, it says Not recorded.
- Previously queued emails retain their original payload for safe delivery
  retries. Re-email a receipt after updating to use the new layout.

No new database tables, npm packages, environment settings or Stripe price
changes are needed for this update. Your current paid add-on and sandbox
settings continue to apply. The installer backs up replaced files and refuses
unknown local edits instead of overwriting them.
