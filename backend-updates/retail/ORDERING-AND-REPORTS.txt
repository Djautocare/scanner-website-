INVENTORYOS RETAIL: ORDERING, PRICE REVIEWS AND REPORTS (v9)

INSTALL ON THE PC THAT RUNS YOUR BACKEND
1. Download InventoryOS-retail-ordering-update.zip and extract it.
2. Put the extracted InventoryOS-retail-ordering-update folder INSIDE your
   current backend folder (the same folder that contains server.js and .env).
   Keep files inside the update folder. Do not manually copy its files folder.
3. Stop the existing InventoryOS Backend task in Task Scheduler. Finish any
   till transactions first. Keep your normal database backup before upgrading.
4. Open PowerShell in your backend folder and run:
   node .\InventoryOS-retail-ordering-update\install.cjs
5. If installation succeeds, run:
   node .\check-retail.cjs
6. If the check passes, start InventoryOS Backend again using your normal task.
7. Refresh the Retail website. Close/reopen any installed Retail or Till app.

No new .env values, npm packages or Stripe prices are required. The check adds
new Retail tables/columns automatically. It does not change your main inventory
stock or historic purchase costs. This is a cumulative Retail code update.

The installer backs up changed files and refuses to overwrite unrecognised
Retail edits. If it stops, keep the message; do not force it or delete live files.
To restore code, stop the backend and run from the backend folder:
   node .\InventoryOS-retail-ordering-update\restore.cjs "FULL BACKUP FOLDER"
Use the exact retail-backup folder printed by installation. Restore does not
remove database tables, stock events, reports or other recorded transactions.

WHERE TO FIND THE NEW FEATURES
Products > Supplier links & prices:
- First save a supplier name and exact PRODUCT link on the product.
- Configure supplier product code, variant (if needed), units in the supplier
  pack, price, VAT basis and optional minimum order value.
- Read code & pack price tries the public product page. Check the size/variant,
  currency, VAT and pack price, then confirm. Manual entry remains available.
- Daily checking is optional and runs while your backend is running. A check
  failure keeps the previous estimate and shows the reason. No price is invented.
- A supplier unit-cost increase creates one dashboard task for all pending
  reviews. Review prices shows the link, cost increase, selling price, unit
  profit impact and recommendation. You choose Apply/edit price or Keep price.
- Recommendation: current selling price x new estimated unit cost / previous
  unit cost, rounded up to a penny. This preserves percentage margin when VAT
  treatment is unchanged. No automatic recommendation if the old cost is missing
  or the current price is at/below it. Consider your own rounding and margins.
- Estimates do not overwrite product purchase cost or previous delivery/sale
  snapshots. Receiving an order still records its confirmed delivery cost.

Reports > Ordering:
- Live reorder quantities grouped by supplier, with codes and product links.
- Copy order list for use on the supplier website. This does not submit a basket
  or purchase anything. You place the actual supplier order yourself.
- Quantities round up to whole supplier packs. Missing costs and supplier
  minimums are flagged; totals are estimates and exclude shipping/discounts.
- Create supplier order records an expected delivery. Outstanding deliveries
  reduce subsequent suggestions to help avoid ordering the same stock twice.

Reports:
- Transactions (sales/refunds/search/original receipt), Daily sales and Waste.
- Included in Retail: Stock-count losses, Biggest waste lines and Management activity.
- Date presets and custom dates use the store timezone; end date is inclusive.
- Ordering is live. Historic snapshots remain in your database.
- Historical counts without a saved cost show unknown/partially priced values;
  they are never repriced using today's product cost. Gross profit is estimated,
  using saved transaction costs and VAT information, not a full accounts report.

Admin > Staff access > Store permission:
- Tick Management controls for people allowed to approve waste at THIS store.
- In Till, open Management controls when the basket is empty. Select product,
  quantity and Out of date or Damaged. The authoriser enters their staff code/QR
  and PIN afresh. Cashier, approver, till, reason and cost are saved.
- An uncertain write-off can be retried in the same form with the same request.
  If you close it, check the Waste report before entering it again.

Stock counts:
- Default large-difference threshold is 10 units OR GBP25 at recorded unit cost.
- A matching second count or explicit owner/admin approval is required before
  stock changes. Adjust these thresholds in Retail settings.

Checkout shows all registered tills with their status. Closed, inactive,
occupied or unlicensed tills remain visible but cannot be chosen for a sale.

SUPPLIER SUPPORT LIMIT
Public GBP pages with a single identifiable structured product offer can be
read. Login/member-only prices, anti-bot pages, ambiguous variants and unsupported
sites need manual entry. Not every supplier exposes usable prices; Costco,
Bookers and other real supplier pages are not guaranteed. No real supplier
compatibility was verified in this release environment. Validate each supplier
with a known product/pack before enabling its daily checks. Private/local URLs,
unsafe redirects and oversized downloads are blocked.

CHECKS PERFORMED
Existing Retail regression suites plus new supplier identity/URL safety,
VAT/pack conversion, price-review/retry, order duplication, recount, fresh-PIN
waste, historic-cost, report date, tenant and subscription-permission tests.
Tests use an isolated PostgreSQL-compatible database and controlled supplier
responses. Your live PostgreSQL, scanner and printer were not accessed.
