TEST INVENTORYOS RETAIL WITHOUT REAL MONEY

First install v12 and complete STRIPE-SETUP.txt. Never share your keys here.

1. Create and verify a SEPARATE InventoryOS test account. Sign in and open Retail.
2. Create a Retail business/first shop, for example 'Test shop'. Do not start
   checkout yet. Creating the shop does not charge or require an override.
3. On your OWNER ADMIN account, open Admin portal > that test account >
   Retail subscriptions & overrides. Choose Test shop.
4. Expand Stripe sandbox testing. Select Sandbox — test payments only.
   Enter a reason (e.g. 'Retail billing test') and Save billing environment.
   This control uses your existing protected administrator checks.
5. Back on the TEST account, open Settings > Manage InventoryOS Retail billing.
   The shop must clearly say SANDBOX — TEST PAYMENTS ONLY.
6. Click Test subscribe — GBP 19.99/month. Confirm, then use Stripe's TEST card:
   Number: 4242 4242 4242 4242
   Expiry: any future month/year
   CVC: any three digits
   Use a test name/address. Do not enter a real card.
7. Complete checkout. After returning, the sandbox shop should show Active,
   its renewal date and GBP 19.99/month in the SANDBOX total. LIVE total stays 0.
   If confirmation is delayed, press Refresh billing and check the sandbox
   webhook delivery in Stripe. Retry any delivery that failed before installation.
8. Create a SECOND test shop in the same Retail business. In your admin account,
   mark that shop sandbox BEFORE its first checkout. Subscribe it with the same
   test card. The sandbox total becomes GBP 39.98/month; live remains unchanged.
9. Test payment details/invoices and Cancel this shop. Stripe asks for final
   confirmation and schedules cancellation at period end. Other shops remain.
10. A declined payment can be tested using 4000 0000 0000 0002.
    A failed/cancelled checkout must not grant paid access or increase totals.

Admin trial/plan overrides can hide whether payment gating works. Clear the
Retail access override on the test shop when testing access before/after payment.
A normal Business trial grants no Retail access. Sandbox subscriptions do not
charge real money, but the shop itself is still a real test data container:
keep test products/receipts separate from your trading records.

The automated backend/browser tests cover billing requests, webhook isolation,
monthly totals, checkout retries, cancellation and ordinary workspace separation.
The real round-trip on your PC still needs the private environment setup and
this account test. A standalone Stripe demo link is a checkout preview only;
it does not activate a shop, because it is not linked to an InventoryOS account.
