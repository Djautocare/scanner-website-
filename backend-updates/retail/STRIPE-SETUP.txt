RETAIL STRIPE SETUP — USE YOUR EXISTING STRIPE ACCOUNT

You can test every Retail entitlement with your admin overrides before this.
Do not send your Stripe secret key or webhook signing secret to ChatGPT.

1. In Stripe, create three MONTHLY recurring GBP prices:
   Retail Starter — GBP 9.99 / month
   Retail Pro — GBP 19.99 / month
   Additional pair of tills — GBP 4.99 / month
   The till-pair price must be normal licensed/per-unit pricing (not usage-based).
   Starter/Pro quantity is always 1; the extra-pair quantity is the number of pairs.

2. Copy the THREE price_... IDs into your BACKEND .env:
   STRIPE_PRICE_RETAIL_STARTER_MONTHLY=price_your_starter_price
   STRIPE_PRICE_RETAIL_PRO_MONTHLY=price_your_pro_price
   STRIPE_PRICE_RETAIL_TILL_PAIR_MONTHLY=price_your_pair_price

   Keep your existing STRIPE_SECRET_KEY and STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET unchanged.
   New Retail checkout explicitly keeps automatic_tax disabled, as requested.
   Never mix test-mode prices with a live-mode secret key (or the reverse).

3. Keep the EXISTING /billing/webhook URL. The installer adds Retail handling
   AFTER signature verification, before standard InventoryOS plan handling.
   It needs the existing events:
   checkout.session.completed
   customer.subscription.created / updated / deleted
   invoice.payment_failed / payment_action_required / payment_succeeded
   Retail subscriptions carry product_type=inventoryos_retail and store metadata.
   They never overwrite a reseller/manufacturing subscription.
   Duplicate/delayed events retrieve current subscription state before updating.

4. Restart the backend, then Retail > Stores, tills & Retail billing.
   Select the store, choose Starter/Pro and optional extra till pairs.
   Only a store owner/workspace admin can buy or change subscriptions.
   Stripe-confirmed state controls paid access; the checkout return page alone
   does not unlock features.
   Updates to paid plans/pairs use prorated invoices and require successful
   payment; failed payment does not grant extra tills.

5. Cancellation is through Manage billing / cancel (Stripe customer portal).
   Enable cancellation in your Stripe portal settings. If using a dedicated
   Retail portal configuration, optionally add its ID:
   STRIPE_RETAIL_PORTAL_CONFIGURATION=bpc_your_retail_portal_configuration
   Normal paid InventoryOS subscriptions remain in their existing billing flow.
   The portal may show other subscriptions for the same Stripe customer.
   Prefer Stores & tills for plan/pair changes so you can deactivate surplus
   till positions before reducing your allowance.

6. Device licences are checked on every till request.
   A canceled/inactive subscription locks tills unless a valid admin trial or
   override remains. A Pro-to-Starter change locks Pro tools, keeping history.
   A valid new upgrade re-enables access; staff/loyalty records are retained.
   Admin overrides do not cancel existing Stripe charges. Cancel paid billing
   through Stripe if a customer should no longer be charged.

7. Before taking a real payment, use Stripe test mode in a separate backend
   test environment. Automated tests in this package mock Stripe and Resend;
   they do not create customers, charge cards or send real emails.
