INVENTORYOS RETAIL — ONE PLAN, £19.99 PER SHOP PER MONTH

1. Keep your existing Stripe account, secret key and webhook.
2. Use ONE active monthly recurring GBP price for £19.99.
   You can reuse your existing £19.99 Retail Pro price.
3. Put its price_... ID in your backend .env:
   STRIPE_PRICE_RETAIL_MONTHLY=price_your_1999_monthly_price
   If omitted, STRIPE_PRICE_RETAIL_PRO_MONTHLY is accepted as a fallback.
   Keep old Starter/Pro/till-pair price IDs while legacy subscriptions exist:
   they are needed to recognise webhooks and remove the old till add-on.
4. Keep your existing /billing/webhook endpoint and subscribed events.
   No new webhook, key, npm package or PostgreSQL table setup is manual.
5. Restart backend. Open Retail > Admin > Stores & billing for each shop.
   One subscription covers that shop, all Retail features and unlimited tills.
   Each extra shop needs its own subscription. Staff and devices are not billed.
6. Existing subscriptions are NOT silently repriced. Until the owner confirms
   switching, existing Stripe charges continue. Confirmation updates the base
   to £19.99 and removes any extra-till item, with a Stripe prorated adjustment.
   Legacy active stores already receive all tools and unlimited till licences.
   Do not archive old Stripe prices until all subscriptions have switched.
7. Admin Retail overrides apply only to the selected shop. A timed Retail grant
   can be used for a free trial. Expiry returns to paid Retail access or locked.
   Overrides never change or cancel Stripe charges.
8. Your ordinary Free/Starter/Pro/Business InventoryOS plans are unchanged.
   Retail shops have separate workspaces, inventory, billing and selection.
9. Test Stripe in a separate test backend before accepting real subscriptions.
   The automated checks mock Stripe and never charge cards or send emails.
