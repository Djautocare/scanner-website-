INVENTORYOS - RETAIL COUNTER & APPEARANCE UPDATE (v5)

Includes the previous Retail releases. Existing data is retained.
No new npm packages, credentials or .env changes are required.

INSTALL ON YOUR BACKEND PC

1. Sync saved offline sales on ALL till devices. Back up PostgreSQL, then stop
   the InventoryOS Backend task.
2. Extract the ZIP. Put the WHOLE InventoryOS-retail-counter-update folder
   inside your backend folder, beside server.js.
3. Open PowerShell in that BACKEND folder and run:

   node .\InventoryOS-retail-counter-update\install.cjs
   node .\check-retail.cjs

4. If the check says PASS, restart your existing backend task. If it fails,
   keep the backend stopped and send the error text, without passwords or .env.
5. Refresh the website (Ctrl+F5 on PC). Prepare offline devices again before
   offline trading. The check creates the extra tables automatically.

THE NEW MENU

Products: Products & stock, Deliveries, Offers, Reductions & waste,
          Shelf labels, Import & export (as applicable to enabled tools).
Customers: Customers & loyalty, Preorders & collections.
Reports: Sales & receipts, Reorder report, Gross profit/staff reports.
Admin: Staff & access, Stock counts (with a due badge), Retail settings,
       Offline & sync.
Dashboard, Checkout, Services, Appointments, Tasks and Till & cash remain
quick links. Physical products and Services have separate lists and forms.
Services only appear when enabled in Retail business settings.

STAFF NUMBER / PIN SETUP

1. Admin > Staff & access > Add staff. Save their name and optional commission.
2. Set number / PIN. Give them a unique number and a 4-8 digit PIN.
3. After setting up staff, tick Require staff PIN login at checkout and Save.
   This starts OFF to preserve existing tills. Sync all offline sales first.
4. Checkout > Staff log in. Each completed sale saves the logged-in cashier,
   even if another staff ID is supplied by an older client. The receipt and
   sales history show the saved cashier. PINs are not recoverable; reset one
   from Staff & access if forgotten. Resetting or disabling staff revokes access.
5. Lock / log out immediately revokes that session; the unpaid basket stays.
   Assign till to me reserves THIS browser's till for that member of staff.
   They can unlock it with their PIN. An owner/admin can Release till.

Staff sessions last up to 12 hours and remain in this tab's session storage.
Five wrong attempts lock PIN login for that app account/inventory for 15 minutes.
Login, assigned-till checkout and staff-attributed sales require a connection.
Existing ordinary offline cash checkout remains available when PIN login is
optional and no staff PIN session is in use.

IMPORTANT: PINs are Retail cashier identities, not new website accounts.
Website/workspace roles still control admin access. On a shared staff till,
use a workspace MEMBER account, not the owner's InventoryOS account. Staff
cannot gain owner/admin permissions merely by entering a PIN. The original
site admin portal and its account restrictions are not changed.

COUNTER TOOLS

- Categories & favourites: edit a product/service, set its category and tick
  Favourite on till. Checkout has category and favourite buttons. Barcode
  scanning still searches the saved catalogue regardless of the selected tab.
- Park a sale: ordinary baskets only. Give it a name, then serve the next
  customer. Parked sales > Recall brings it back at CURRENT prices. Parking
  does not reserve or deduct stock, take cash, or hold an old offer price.
  Linked bookings/preorders/customer rewards should be completed or detached
  first. Another device can recall a parked basket once.
- Age checks: set Minimum age on a product (0 = unrestricted). Adding it asks
  staff to complete the age check or refuse the sale. Recalled baskets require
  fresh confirmation. This records the staff acknowledgement; the shop still
  decides which products need checks and how staff verify age.
- Partial returns: Reports > Sales & receipts > Return / exchange. Choose only
  returned quantities, and tick which are saleable to put back into stock.
  Discounts and VAT use the original sale values, with cumulative penny
  allocation. The original receipt remains intact. Each return gets a receipt.
- Exchanges: add replacement items in that same return screen. The preview
  shows return credit, new total, additional cash due and change. Cash, stock
  and both records commit together. Only completed original quantities can be
  returned. Refunds/exchanges are owner/admin actions and currently cash only.
  Loyalty earned on the original sale is reversed at the first return; spent
  stamps are restored once all its goods are returned. Tips are optional.
- Import & export: download the CSV template, fill it, then Preview import.
  Nothing is saved until Confirm import. A matching barcode updates product
  details without replacing existing stock. Opening quantities apply only to
  NEW products. Up to 2,000 rows / 1 MB, physical products only. Services have
  their own creation screen. Exported formula-like text is escaped for safety.
- Shelf labels: select products and 2 x 1 or 4 x 6 inch paper. Create labels,
  Download PDF / Mobile share, or open the PDF to print. Labels include name,
  current selling price and a scannable Code 128 barcode. Print at 100% / actual
  size with the matching thermal label size. Up to 50 labels per PDF.

LIGHT / DARK MODE

Use Account settings > Appearance, or Retail settings > Appearance.
Light has a white background; Dark keeps the black background. Your accent
colour remains selected, with a darker shade in Light for readable text.
Appearance persists across application pages and tabs on this browser/device.
Accent colour continues to use the existing account setting. Other devices
can choose their own light/dark appearance.

ROLLBACK

Keep the retail-backup-... folder printed by the installer. The included
restore.cjs can restore unchanged code from that backup; it does not delete
new database records or reverse completed sales. Sync device queues first.
If rolling back after partial returns have been recorded, keep the v5 backend
for financial reporting: older versions do not understand partial returns.

PREVIOUS BUSINESS TOOLS / MANUFACTURING / OFFLINE REFERENCE

SWITCH ON THE TOOLS YOU NEED

Retail > Retail settings > Business type & tools.
Choose General shop, Corner shop, Barber / salon or Bakery. Press Apply
recommended tools, adjust the individual switches, then Save business tools.
These choices belong to the selected Retail inventory. Turning a tool off
retains its records. VAT and receipt business details stay workspace-wide.

- Services: add an item with Item type = Service. It has a price, estimated cost
  and duration, but does not receive stock or deduct stock when sold.
- Appointments: book a service or add a walk-in, optionally choose a customer
  and staff member. Start the service, then Take payment to complete it.
  Record a cash deposit with an open till if required. Deposits are applied
  once at checkout; cancelled bookings require an explicit deposit refund.
- Email reminders: only sent when requested for a booking. Existing backend
  RESEND_API_KEY and EMAIL_FROM settings are used; no new key is needed if
  your existing app emails work. The backend must be running. Reminders are
  service messages, not mailing-list signups. Use real times and consent.
- Staff: add staff and optional commission percentage, choose staff and tips
  at checkout. Commission is a reporting estimate; it is not payroll.
- Cases: set a product's units per case. Stock in Cases or use Cases on a
  delivery line; the system converts them to individual sellable units.
- Offers: create percentage discounts, same-product multibuys or meal deals.
  Priority decides which deal uses a unit first; one unit cannot be used by
  two offers. Timed reductions set the starting price before offers.
- Preorders: reserve current stock or add a Make / production order. Take an
  optional cash deposit, mark it ready when finished stock is available, then
  Collect & pay. Collection deducts stock and applies the deposit once.
  Agreed preorder prices are preserved; new offers are not applied to them.
- Production list: linked recipes can be opened in Manufacturing Calculate.
  This is a planning handoff. Manufacturing and Retail stock remain separate:
  receive finished products into Retail once before marking a make order ready.
  It does not automatically transfer or duplicate finished stock.
- Customers & loyalty: save a customer and show/print their QR card. Set the
  stamp target, minimum spend and free reward in Retail settings. Select/scan
  the customer at checkout, and select Redeem when their balance qualifies.
  Refunds reverse stamps; a previously spent stamp may leave a negative balance.
- Reductions & waste: schedule reduced prices and record unsold, expired or
  damaged units. Waste removes available stock with an audit record and cost.
- Gross profit: owner/admin reports compare actual discounted revenue after
  VAT with saved costs. Tips are separate; overheads and payroll are excluded.
- Customer display: Checkout > Open customer display. Move that second tab to
  another screen connected to the SAME browser on the SAME device. It shows
  basket, total and change only; customer contact details are never broadcast.
  It is not a remote screen link for a separate phone/tablet/browser.

MANUFACTURING

Manufacturing now opens in its own workspace with Retail-style navigation,
colours and cards, on desktop and mobile. Existing links redirect there.
Existing Business-plan checks and the Manufacturing setting still apply.
Your original materials, recipes, production, batches, equipment and timers
use the same APIs and data. This installer does not replace those modules.

BARCODE LOOKUP FOR THE WHOLE SHOP

Scan or type an EAN/UPC in Add product, then Look up barcode. Searches route
across Open Food Facts (including alcohol), Open Beauty Facts (toiletries),
Open Products Facts (household/general goods) and Open Pet Food Facts.
Check the name, brand and pack size before accepting; set your own price,
cost, stock and VAT treatment. These are public catalogues, not a complete
list of every product. If no match exists, enter the name once and save it
against its barcode; your shop will recognise that saved item next time.
Public lookup requires internet. Checkout scans your saved shop products.
USB scanners should use Keyboard / HID mode with an Enter or Tab suffix.

ONLINE / OFFLINE

Ordinary cash sales of saved stock products and services can queue offline
using the prepared, signed prices/promotions. Staff attribution, tips,
customer loyalty, bookings, preorders and deposits need a working connection.
Cash deposits increase till cash when taken but are not sales revenue until
collection. Held deposits need an explicit refund with an open till.
No integrated card payments, cash-drawer hardware or external POS sync are
included. Use your existing external card system separately.

PREPARE OFFLINE CASH CHECKOUT ON EACH DEVICE

1. Sign in while connected. Pick exactly one workspace/inventory.
2. Add the products and prices you need, then open the till with a counted float.
3. Open Offline & sync > Prepare / refresh device. Allow persistent device
   storage if the browser asks. Wait for the screen to confirm saved Retail
   screens and checkout authorisation. Check it before trading offline.
4. Checkout works with saved products for up to 24 hours after preparation.
   Cash sales are durably saved to this device BEFORE the receipt appears.
   Receipts identify sales awaiting server confirmation.
5. Keep using the same browser/account/device. When internet/backend returns,
   the current inventory's queue retries automatically. Offline & sync > Sync
   now also retries. Check that no sales remain awaiting sync before closing
   the till, changing device, clearing browser data or updating the app.
6. Download saved sales backup if a sale needs attention. A backup preserves
   the original details for reconciliation; it is not a second checkout and
   should not be manually submitted under a new request ID.

OFFLINE BOUNDARIES

- This release supports OFFLINE CASH SALES and viewing saved Retail screens.
  Product edits/new products, name lookups, deliveries, stock counts, refunds,
  settings and till opening/cash management require a working connection.
- The Retail page must have been prepared online. Other InventoryOS pages and
  a new login are not available offline. External card payments are not added.
- Pending sales belong to the account/workspace/inventory that saved them.
  Sign in to that same account and select that inventory to sync its queue.
- The backend rechecks current membership/role, plan and Retail enablement on
  sync. It verifies signed price, tax, seller and till snapshots. Stock is
  deducted from CURRENT stock, never replaced by an old device quantity.
- The same request ID is retried after a lost response; no duplicate sale is
  created. Sale time and upload time are retained separately.
- A changed/closed till, removed access, expired authorisation or other conflict
  stays in the queue for review. It is never silently dropped or attached to a
  different till. Contact the workspace owner to reconcile a blocked sale.
- Offline authorisation permits sales within 24 hours, with a seven-day upload
  window. Reconnect promptly. An already-confirmed request can still be safely
  recovered after expiry.
- Private browsing and clearing/evicting site storage can lose device data.
  Do not clear storage while sales are pending. Storage persistence is a
  browser preference, not a guarantee. Download a backup if sync is blocked.
- An open page syncs after reconnecting. If all tabs are closed, reopen Retail
  in the same browser/account/inventory to resume; it is not a background server.
- The backend remains required for reports, shared data and other devices.
  Daily reorder snapshots are generated only while the backend is running.

ROLLBACK

Keep the retail-backup-... folder printed by install.cjs. Code can be restored
with the included restore.cjs and that backup path, provided those files have
not since been changed. Saved database records are never deleted by restore.
Sync all saved device sales before changing or restoring the installed version.
