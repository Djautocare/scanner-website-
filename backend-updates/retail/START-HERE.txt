INVENTORYOS RETAIL UPDATE - VAT, RECEIPT NAME AND PHONE BARCODE SETUP

This upgrades the Retail version you already installed. Existing stock, sales,
settings and old receipts are kept. No new npm packages, API key or .env changes.

1. Make your normal PostgreSQL backup and stop InventoryOS Backend.

2. Extract the ZIP. Put the complete InventoryOS-retail-vat-barcode-update
   folder inside your usual backend folder, next to server.js.
   Do not manually copy the individual files over your backend.

3. Open PowerShell in your BACKEND folder. Run:

   node .\InventoryOS-retail-vat-barcode-update\install.cjs
   node .\check-retail.cjs

   The installer backs up the files it changes. It recognises your previous
   installation, keeps the existing server mount/start lines, and adds the new
   lookup helper. The check adds the required database columns automatically.

4. If the check says PASS, restart your existing InventoryOS Backend task.
   If either command fails, send the error text before continuing. Keep your
   .env, passwords and database credentials private.

5. Refresh the website. If it looks unchanged, press Ctrl+F5 on PC or reload
   the page on your phone after the website deployment has finished.

RECEIPT NAME AND VAT

- Open Retail > Retail settings > Receipt details.
- Enter Business name on receipts. It prints at the top of new receipts even
  with VAT turned off. You may also enter the business address.
- Only VAT-registered shops should turn on VAT receipt. Enter the registered
  business name, address and VAT registration number, then save settings.
- Edit existing products and choose their VAT treatment. New products have a
  VAT applicable checkbox for 20% or 5%; when unticked, choose zero-rated,
  exempt or outside VAT scope as appropriate. Lookup does not determine tax.
- Prices ALREADY INCLUDE VAT. A GBP 1.20 item at 20% stays GBP 1.20: GBP 1.00
  net plus GBP 0.20 VAT. It does not become GBP 1.44.
- The server calculates VAT on each sale line, rounds to the nearest penny
  with halves up, and sums those amounts into the rate breakdown.
- VAT must be classified before a product can be sold while VAT receipts are
  enabled. Existing products are not automatically assigned a tax rate.
- Receipts save their original seller details and tax breakdown. Editing the
  settings or product afterwards does not rewrite old receipts. Sales from
  before this update are not retrospectively assigned VAT or a new shop name.
- This is a UK domestic sales receipt with a VAT breakdown. Full VAT invoices,
  credit notes, VAT returns, margin schemes and other special VAT schemes are
  not generated. Provide a full VAT invoice separately when required, including
  transactions above GBP 250 or when requested. Check the applicable tax
  treatment for the actual products your customer sells.

WALK AROUND A SHOP ADDING PRODUCTS

1. Sign into the customer's authorised workspace on your PHONE. Pick the exact
   Retail inventory being set up; only the workspace owner/admin can add items.
2. Open Retail > Products & stock > Scan to add product.
3. Allow camera permission. Point the rear camera at the full EAN barcode and
   hold still in good light. It needs two matching valid reads.
4. If Open Food Facts has it, check the name and pack size, then tap
   Use these details. This fills the name and keeps the EAN.
5. Enter that shop's selling price, cost, opening stock, minimum and reorder
   target. Set the correct VAT treatment if the shop uses VAT receipts.
6. Tap Save and scan next to move straight to the next item.

You can also type a barcode and tap Look up barcode, or use Add product and
enter the name manually if no match exists. Duplicate barcodes are flagged;
stock in or edit the existing product instead of creating another copy.

Open Food Facts identifies groceries by exact barcode, normally returning one
product match rather than a list of different products. A barcode can identify
a particular pack size/flavour, so confirm it against the packaging. Coverage
is incomplete, and it does not provide your customer's selling prices or
reliable UK VAT classifications. Non-food items may need manual entry.

The phone decodes video locally. Camera frames are not uploaded. Only the
barcode goes to your backend and Open Food Facts. Scanner code is served from
InventoryOS, including a local ZXing fallback for browsers without the native
barcode detector. The camera stops on a match, close, page hide or timeout.

Lookup results are public product data, briefly cached in backend memory.
Each saved product stays in the customer's private workspace/inventory. This
update does not publish a shared catalogue, share customer prices/stock, or
upload changes to Open Food Facts.

The upstream API has usage limits. The backend caches matches and limits
requests; if it asks you to wait, continue manually or retry after a minute.
Camera scanning needs HTTPS and a supported camera/browser. A real phone's
camera quality/permissions should be checked during the first shop setup.

Open Food Facts data is under ODbL, with attribution and share-alike conditions.
The app preserves its source attribution. Review those terms before exporting,
redistributing or building a shared catalogue from that data.
https://world.openfoodfacts.org

CODE RESTORE IF NEEDED

Stop the backend. Use the exact retail-backup-... folder printed by the installer:

   node .\InventoryOS-retail-vat-barcode-update\restore.cjs .\retail-backup-YOUR-FOLDER-NAME

This restores only unchanged installer-managed code and installation metadata.
It keeps PostgreSQL stock/sales and additive columns. Restart the backend.
Keep the backup until you are happy with the update.

Card/contactless terminals, cash-drawer hardware and external POS sync remain
unconnected. Existing cash checkout, deliveries, stock counts and till controls
continue to work as before.
