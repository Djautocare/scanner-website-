INVENTORYOS RETAIL — STORE DELETION (v16)

1. Extract the whole InventoryOS-retail-store-deletion-update folder into your
   backend folder, beside server.js. Do not copy individual files by hand.
2. Finish transactions and sync saved offline sales, then stop the backend using
   your usual working method.
3. Open PowerShell in the backend folder and run:

   node .\InventoryOS-retail-store-deletion-update\install.cjs
   node .\check-retail.cjs
   node .\check-retail-store-deletion.cjs

4. If both checks pass, restart your backend and refresh/reopen Retail.

No new .env settings or npm packages are required. Your existing Resend sender
and API key are used. The checks do not send emails, delete data or change Stripe.
The installer creates a code backup and adds its PostgreSQL table automatically
when a check starts the Retail schema. Keep your normal database backups too.

HOW TO REMOVE A STORE

Only its Retail business owner can delete it. Open Admin > Stores & billing,
select the unused store, and choose Delete store near the bottom.

Finish transactions, log staff out of drawers and balance/close the store's tills.
The screen lists anything that needs finishing. Sync every offline device first.
Choose Email my CSV backup. It goes only to the account email used to sign in,
looked up on the server. Save every attachment before continuing.

Tick the box confirming the emailed backup has been received and saved. Type
Confirm delete exactly, then press Permanently delete store.

Products have their own products.csv. The email includes separate CSVs for sales,
sale lines, refunds, customers, stock history, counts, orders/delivery lines,
cash/tills, staff assignments, appointments, loyalty, wastage, tasks, reports,
settings and other store business records. backup-index.csv lists every file.
Money columns ending _pence are GBP pence. Nested settings/receipt snapshots are
JSON within CSV cells. Import barcode columns as Text to preserve leading zeroes.
Spreadsheet formulas in text fields are escaped for safe opening.

Staff PINs, account passwords, QR/login tokens and temporary authentication caches
are never emailed. Your account, shared Retail business, Help history and other
stores remain. Staff assigned to another store retain their access there.

BILLING AND FAILURE HANDLING

Deleting stops renewal of the store's Retail and email-receipt subscriptions at
the current billing period's end. It does not refund payments, erase invoices or
waive usage already incurred. Final email-receipt usage can still be invoiced.
Open, unpaid checkout sessions are expired; a newly completed payment requires
billing to be refreshed and a new backup before deletion.

If email, billing or database validation fails, store data is retained. If Stripe
renewals were already stopped before a later failure, that cancellation remains;
the screen explains this. A timed-out confirmed deletion can be safely retried.

Backups expire after 23 hours. A change to store data requires a fresh backup.
Email acceptance is checked by the server; actual receipt must also be confirmed
by the owner. Temporary backup email payloads are removed on successful deletion
or expiry. Minimal deletion/billing references remain for safe retries and Stripe
webhooks; shared workspace idempotency records remain to prevent repeated sales.

If the full encoded backup exceeds 28 MB, deletion is blocked and support must
arrange the full backup. No truncated backup is sent. Unknown additional store
tables with records also block deletion until they can be included safely.

INSTALLER SAFETY

This includes previous Retail updates. Your .env, original Help service and
protected admin middleware are retained. The installer stops on unrecognised
local edits instead of overwriting them. Do not bypass that protection.
Restoring old code cannot undo a completed deletion or a Stripe cancellation.
