InventoryOS walkthrough

Start with the everyday tools included with InventoryOS, then move through the paid tiers when you are ready. Each section shows where to find the feature and what it is for.

1. Free foundationTrack stock, locations, sales and expenses.
2. Starter toolsSpeed up labels, receipts and dispatch.
3. Pro workflowsHandle larger, more advanced operations.
4. Business teamsShare workspaces and manage people.
1
Free foundationSet up your workspace and learn the daily stock workflow.
Included
1

Dashboard

See your current stock, locations, sales and reminders at a glance.

Summary cards
Find it: Dashboard in the left menu.
2

Set up locations

Choose whether your locations are called boxes, shelves, racks or something custom.

Location wording
Find it: Settings → Workspace Settings.
3

Add stock

Scan or type a product, enter the quantity and choose the saved location. The raw location value is kept safely in the background.

Save stock
Find it: Add stock in the left menu.
4

Manage and find stock

Search items, move stock between locations, remove sold stock, record broken items and process refunds.

Choose an action
Find it: Search stock, Move stock, Remove stock, Discard stock and Refunds.
5

Reports and housekeeping

Use Sales Tracker for performance, Expenses for costs and Stale Stock to find items that need attention.

Reports
Find it: Reports in the left menu.
2
Starter featuresFor casual sellers ready to add receipts, labels and dispatch workflows.
Starter
1

Barcode printing

Print product and location labels for your physical storage system, then scan them during stock movements.

Print labels
Find it: Barcode Printing under Operations.
2

Dispatch Centre

Bring shipping labels into one queue, work through the packing order and confirm each item before dispatch.

Packing queue
Find it: Dispatch Centre under Operations.
3

Corrections

Correct quantities, product assignments, locations and sale details while keeping an activity record.

Find it: Corrections under Operations.
Your Billing & Plans page is the source of truth for the exact features and limits included in your current plan.
3
Pro featuresFor serious resellers managing more stock, labels and multiple inventories.
Pro
1

Higher-capacity workflows

Work with larger stock volumes and more involved product, location and label routines without changing your everyday process.

Best starting point: Review your current plan from the plan button in the top bar.
2

Advanced label and shipping work

Use the shipping and label tools together so incoming labels, locations and dispatch checks stay connected.

Best starting point: Open Dispatch Centre, then follow the label and packing prompts.
3

Multiple inventory workflows

Use the inventory selector in the top bar to switch between the inventories available to your workspace.

Find it: Inventory selector at the top of the page.
Pro access is controlled by your account and workspace billing status. If a feature is locked, use the plan button in the top bar to review the available upgrade.
4
Business featuresFor teams running inventory together across shared workspaces.
Business
1

Shared workspaces

Keep a team’s inventory together while separating workspaces for different businesses, storage areas or workflows.

Find it: Workspace selector in the top bar.
2

Members and permissions

Invite colleagues, assign workspace roles and remove access when someone no longer needs it.

Find it: Settings → Workspace Members.
3

Team-ready administration

Use the workspace controls and plan management tools to keep access, billing and responsibilities clear.

Find it: Settings and the plan button in the top bar.
